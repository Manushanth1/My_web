Privacy Policy — Roll Ball

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Roll Ball does not collect anything about you. Your progress never leaves your phone. The one exception is advertising: the game shows a small number of ads, and Google's advertising software — not us — collects information in order to choose and measure them.

There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no in-app purchases.

What this policy covers

The Android application published as Roll Ball, package name com.bouncehero.game. The package name predates the game's current title and is kept because changing it would publish a different app rather than an update to this one; it is the same software described here.

What the game itself stores

Everything the game saves is stored on your device only. There is no account, no server, and no sync. It consists of:

  • which levels you have cleared, your best times, your badges and achievements;
  • your settings (volume, vibration, motion, contrast, colour-vision mode, touch control layout, assist mode);
  • totals: time played, number of deaths, number of level clears.

None of it is transmitted anywhere. It is held in the app's own private storage (WebView local storage, mirrored into Android SharedPreferences so that clearing the app's cache does not erase your progress).

Deleting it: Settings → Data → Erase removes all of it immediately. Uninstalling the app also removes it. There is no copy anywhere else, which also means there is nothing to restore it from.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. There are two, and only two, places they can appear:

  • a full-screen ad after finishing a level, at most once every few levels;
  • an optional video you can choose to watch to skip a level you are stuck on.

Ads never appear while you are playing a level.

To select and measure these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier);
  • device information: model, operating system version, screen size, language, coarse location derived from your IP address;
  • app activity: that an ad was requested, shown, or interacted with.

This is collected by Google's software running inside the app. We do not receive it, we cannot see it, and we do not combine it with anything else. What we can see is aggregate, anonymous reporting in the AdMob dashboard: how many ads were shown and what they earned.

Google's handling of this data is governed by their own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob & AdSense policies

Your choices about ads

  • Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or a US state with applicable privacy law, the game asks for your choices about ad personalisation the first time it runs, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Data → Ad privacy choices.
  • Non-personalised by default. Unless you have specifically consented to personalised ads, the game requests non-personalised ads. These still use your device's advertising ID for frequency capping and fraud prevention.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID. Android → Settings → Privacy → Ads. You can reset the ID or delete it entirely; deleting it stops apps from receiving one at all.
  • Offline. The game is fully playable with no network connection. With no connection, no ad is requested and nothing is transmitted.

Permissions

The app asks for four:

PermissionWhy
INTERNETRequesting ads. The game's own content is inside the app and is never downloaded.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEThe ads SDK checks whether there is a connection before making a request.
AD_IDReading the advertising ID, for ads.
VIBRATEHaptic feedback. Can be turned off in Settings → Play → Vibration.

Google's advertising library adds a few more of its own, all of them "normal" permissions that Android grants without asking you and that give no access to anything personal: ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS (Android's Privacy Sandbox advertising APIs), and FOREGROUND_SERVICE and WAKE_LOCK (used by Google Play services). They are listed here for completeness rather than because they do anything you would notice.

There is no access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, files, photos, or any other app.

Children

Roll Ball is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through this app, contact us at the address below and we will act on it.
